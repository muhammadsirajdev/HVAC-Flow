**Plan terms and footnotes (from the Maintenance Plans page):**
- Pricing covers one residential system unless stated otherwise. Additional systems must be at the same service location.
- Maintenance visits are scheduled in designated off-peak seasons.
- Repair discounts apply to qualifying parts and labor and cannot be combined with other offers unless stated.
- Elite filter replacement covers only standard disposable filters. Specialty, media, electronic, UV and other premium filters are excluded.
- The Elite $0 diagnostic applies during regular business hours to covered systems only. It does not include repairs, parts, refrigerant, materials or additional services.
- After-hours and weekend service is subject to applicable after-hours or weekend pricing.
- The Elite replacement benefit is capped at $500 off.
- "Maintenance plan benefits are subject to applicable plan terms and conditions."
- Not stated on website: contract length, auto-renewal, cancellation or refund terms for plans.

## What the 15-point inspection in MD Cold Services maintenance plans checks
All three maintenance plans include the same 15-point inspection:
1. Blower motor and operation
2. Electrical connections and wiring
3. Capacitor condition and performance
4. Contactor and electrical components
5. Voltage and amperage of applicable components
6. Air filter condition
7. Supply and return temperature differential
8. Evaporator coil condition (where accessible)
9. Condenser coil condition
10. Condensate drain and drain pan
11. Thermostat operation
12. Compressor operation
13. Refrigerant performance and operating pressures (when indicated)
14. System performance evaluation, with any recommended repairs identified
15. Safety controls and overall operation
