# AC Installation & Replacement Service – MD Cold Services

## What the MD Cold Services AC Installation & Replacement service covers
MD Cold Services provides "Professional AC installation and replacement with energy-efficient solutions for South Florida homes and businesses." The service covers residential and commercial properties.

The service includes:
- evaluating the property and the existing system to help decide equipment and sizing
- installing energy-efficient systems from major brands
- removing the existing equipment during replacements

## What is included in an MD Cold Services AC installation or replacement
- "Free In-Home Replacement Estimates"
- "High-Efficiency AC System Options Available"
- "Proper Load Calculation & System Sizing"
- "Flexible Financing Options Available for Qualified Customers"
- "Manufacturer Warranty Coverage Available on Select Systems"
- "Professional Removal & Disposal of Old Equipment"

## Price of an AC installation or replacement at MD Cold Services
Not stated on website. Replacement estimates are free and done in the home. Elite Comfort maintenance plan members get 5% off a qualifying new HVAC system replacement, up to $500 (see 05_pricing.md).

## Warranty on AC installation by MD Cold Services
The website says "Manufacturer Warranty Coverage Available on Select Systems." The website does not state warranty lengths or a separate labor/workmanship warranty.

## Financing for a new AC system from MD Cold Services
Financing is available for qualified customers through Synchrony, Ygrene and Wisetack, subject to approval (see 08_booking_and_payment.md).

- Licensed and insured (FL License #CAC1824837)

- Residential and commercial
- 100% satisfaction guarantee
