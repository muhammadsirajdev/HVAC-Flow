# Commercial HVAC Services – MD Cold Services

## What the MD Cold Services Commercial HVAC service covers
MD Cold Services provides commercial heating, cooling and maintenance for businesses, property managers and commercial facilities across South Florida. MD Cold Services lists four commercial service categories:
- Rooftop Units (RTUs)
- Package Units
- VRF/VRV Systems
- Commercial Ductwork

The Maintenance Plans page also lists split systems and heat pumps as commercial equipment serviced.

## MD Cold Services commercial rooftop units (RTUs)
MD Cold Services services RTUs for retail properties, offices, warehouses, restaurants and other commercial facilities. RTU work covers system diagnostics, repairs, equipment replacement and preventive maintenance.
- "RTU Diagnostics, Repair & Economizer Service"
- "Full Unit Replacement & Crane Lifting Coordination"
- "Scheduled Preventive Maintenance Agreements"

## MD Cold Services commercial package units
MD Cold Services handles installation, repair, replacement and preventive maintenance of packaged systems for restaurants, retail properties, offices and warehouses.
- "Commercial Package Unit Diagnostics & Repair"
- "Package Unit Installation & Replacement"
- "Airflow & Blower System Service"
- "Preventive Maintenance & System Inspections"

## MD Cold Services VRF/VRV multi-zone systems
MD Cold Services installs, diagnoses, repairs and maintains VRF/VRV systems. These systems give individual temperature control for multiple zones from one central system. They suit offices, medical facilities, retail spaces, hospitality properties and other multi-zone sites.
- "VRF/VRV System Diagnostics & Repair"
- "VRF/VRV Installation & Replacement"
- "Daikin & Mitsubishi VRF/VRV Systems"
- "Multi-Zone Temperature Control"
- "Commercial Preventive Maintenance"

## MD Cold Services commercial ductwork
MD Cold Services installs, repairs, replaces and modifies commercial ductwork, and performs airflow balancing. The work uses sheet-metal ductwork.
- "Commercial Ductwork Installation & Replacement"
- "Ductwork Repair & Modifications"
- "Sheet-Metal Ductwork"
- "Airflow & Air Distribution Improvements"
- "Commercial Air Balancing"

## Pricing for MD Cold Services commercial service calls and diagnostics
Commercial service-call and diagnostic charges vary by equipment type and scope. MD Cold Services confirms pricing before dispatch. Diagnostic and repair requests cover RTUs, package units, VRF/VRV systems and other commercial equipment. Installation, replacement and project estimates are handled separately. The website lists no fixed commercial prices.

## MD Cold Services commercial maintenance agreements
MD Cold Services commercial maintenance agreements are tailored to each facility and its equipment. They include:
- customized maintenance schedules
- preventive inspections
- priority service / priority response times
- detailed service and inspection reports

Stated benefits are extended equipment life and reduced downtime. Customers request a quote through the "Request a Commercial Maintenance Consultation" button, which goes to the Housecall Pro booking page. The website lists no fixed price for commercial agreements.
