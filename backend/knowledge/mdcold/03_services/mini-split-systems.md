# Ductless Mini Split Systems – MD Cold Services

## What the MD Cold Services Mini Split service covers
"MD Cold Services installs, repairs, replaces, and services single-zone and multi-zone ductless mini-split systems." Mini-splits provide individual temperature control without traditional ductwork.

Mini-splits can be used for:
- additions
- garages
- individual rooms
- offices
- other residential or commercial spaces
