# MD Cold Services – Booking, Payment and Financing

## How to book a service with MD Cold Services
Every page says: "Book online or call our 24/7 service line to schedule your appointment." There are three ways to book:
1. **Book online** through Housecall Pro: https://book.housecallpro.com/book/MD-Cold-Services/40f25dd932f94ff4bbe5c0b1277c4c04?v2=true
2. **Call** (786) 797-6193. This is the 24/7 line and the recommended option for urgent or emergency service.
3. **Contact form or email** (mdcoldservices@gmail.com) for non-urgent requests or estimates. "Our team will follow up with you."

The Housecall Pro booking page loads its options with JavaScript, so its services, time slots and fields could not be read. The website does not say whether a deposit is required to book.

## What happens after submitting a request to MD Cold Services
After a service request is submitted, the confirmation page says: "Thank you! Your request has been submitted successfully. We'll be in touch shortly." The website gives no specific response time.

## Free estimates from MD Cold Services
MD Cold Services offers "Free In-Home Replacement Estimates" for AC system replacement. The Attic Insulation page invites customers to "Get a free estimate." The Contact page mentions requesting "a free estimate for a system replacement."

## Requesting a commercial maintenance consultation from MD Cold Services
Commercial customers request a maintenance agreement through the "Request a Commercial Maintenance Consultation" button, which goes to the Housecall Pro booking page.

## Payment methods accepted by MD Cold Services
Not stated on website. The website does not list accepted payment methods (cash, card, check and so on).

## MD Cold Services financing options – overview
MD Cold Services offers financing through three partners, "subject to approval and eligibility": Synchrony, Ygrene and Wisetack. The website does not state APR, interest rates, loan terms in months, minimum amounts, down payments or credit criteria.

## MD Cold Services financing partner: Synchrony
- Synchrony Financial is labelled "Primary Approved Partner".
- Synchrony covers HVAC repairs, replacements and new system installations on qualifying residential projects.
- Synchrony is best for homeowners financing larger HVAC repairs, replacements or new installations.
- Promotional financing may be available. Multiple payment options depend on eligibility.
- Apply: "Apply Via Synchrony" – https://www.synchrony.com/mmc/S6244611107?sitecode=acewel401

## MD Cold Services financing partner: Ygrene (PACE)
- Ygrene is labelled "Approved Partner".
- Ygrene offers property-based (PACE) financing for qualifying HVAC upgrades, energy-efficiency improvements and home improvement projects.
- Eligibility considers property value and available equity.
- Long-term financing may extend up to 20 years.
- Repayment is made through an assessment on the property.
- More information: https://ygrene.com/

## MD Cold Services financing partner: Wisetack
- Wisetack is labelled "Approved Partner".
- Wisetack offers payment-based financing for HVAC repairs, replacements and new system installations.
- Wisetack is best for customers who want to finance a project instead of paying upfront.
- Flexible payment options are available to qualifying customers, and the application is described as simple.
- To apply, customers ask MD Cold Services through the Contact page.

## How financing works with MD Cold Services (4 steps)
1. **Request an Estimate:** the team evaluates your HVAC needs and recommends options.
2. **Explore Financing Options:** review the partner programs and choose the best fit.
3. **Apply With Financing Provider:** complete the provider's application to see eligibility and terms.
4. **Schedule Your HVAC Project:** once the project and financing are finalized, the team coordinates service or installation.
