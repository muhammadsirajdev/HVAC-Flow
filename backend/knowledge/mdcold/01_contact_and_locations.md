## Areas MD Cold Services serves (overview)
MD Cold Services serves three regions of South Florida with residential and commercial HVAC: Miami-Dade County, Broward County and the Upper Florida Keys. The Service Areas page lists main cities per region and adds "surrounding cities" or "surrounding areas". The website does not list ZIP codes.

## MD Cold Services service area: Miami-Dade County
The Miami-Dade County page lists 21 areas served:
Miami, Doral, Kendall, Coral Gables, Hialeah, Hialeah Gardens, Homestead, Florida City, Palmetto Bay, Cutler Bay, Pinecrest, South Miami, West Miami, Sweetwater, Miami Lakes, Miami Gardens, Miami Beach, North Miami, North Miami Beach, Aventura, Sunny Isles Beach.

## MD Cold Services service area: Broward County
The Broward County page lists 11 areas served:
Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Miramar, Pembroke Pines, Davie, Plantation, Sunrise, Weston, Cooper City, Hallandale Beach, Coral Springs.

The Broward County page metadata says "Same-day service available."

## MD Cold Services service area: Upper Florida Keys (Monroe County)
The Upper Florida Keys page lists 4 areas served: Key Largo, Tavernier, Islamorada, Marathon.

The page describes "Professional HVAC repair, installation, and maintenance for homes and businesses throughout the Upper Florida Keys" and "Licensed HVAC contractor, fast dispatch."

The website does not state any extra trip fee or different pricing for the Keys.

## Is my city covered by MD Cold Services?
If a city is not in the lists above, the website does not confirm coverage. The Service Areas page only says "surrounding cities" or "surrounding areas". The customer should call (786) 797-6193 to confirm.
