# MD Cold Services – Hours and Emergency Availability

## MD Cold Services emergency availability
MD Cold Services offers 24/7 emergency HVAC service, described as "On-call & after hours". The 24/7 line is (786) 797-6193. The AC Repair page says "24/7 emergency availability is offered throughout the service area. Same-day service is available when scheduling permits."

## MD Cold Services regular business hours
Not stated on website. The website gives no opening hours for any weekday or weekend. Some pages mention "regular business hours" (Elite plan $0 diagnostic) and "after-hours and weekend pricing", but the website never says what those hours are.

## MD Cold Services holiday hours
Not stated on website.

## Do after-hours or emergency visits cost more at MD Cold Services?
Yes, they can. The AC Repair page says: "Additional charges may apply for after-hours or emergency service." The Maintenance Plans page says after-hours and weekend service is subject to applicable after-hours or weekend pricing. The website does not state the amounts.

## How fast can MD Cold Services come out?
The website advertises "Same/Next-Day Service" with "Fast dispatch". Same-day service is "available when scheduling permits". Maintenance plan members get priority scheduling (see 05_pricing.md). The website gives no guaranteed arrival time.
