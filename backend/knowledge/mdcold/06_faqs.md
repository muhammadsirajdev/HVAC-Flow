# MD Cold Services – Frequently Asked Questions

Note: the questions are copied exactly from the site. Some longer answers were captured as close paraphrases (see 99_coverage_report.md).

## Q: How do I know if my AC needs repair?
Service: AC Repair (MD Cold Services)
A: Common signs include warm air from the vents, weak airflow, unusual noises, frequent cycling, water leaks, higher-than-normal energy bills, or the AC failing to hold the desired temperature.

## Q: What types of AC problems do you repair?
Service: AC Repair (MD Cold Services)
A: MD Cold Services repairs refrigerant leaks, electrical and component issues, clogged drain lines, frozen coils, thermostat problems, airflow issues, and systems that are not cooling properly.

## Q: Do you service all major AC brands?
Service: AC Repair (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. MD Cold Services covers most major residential and commercial brands, including Carrier, Rheem, Trane, Goodman, Daikin, Mitsubishi, Lennox, and others.

## Q: Do you provide emergency AC repair?
Service: AC Repair (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. MD Cold Services offers 24/7 emergency availability throughout its service area. Same-day service is available when scheduling permits.

## Q: How much is an AC diagnostic service call?
Service: AC Repair (MD Cold Services)
A: The MD Cold Services residential AC diagnostic is $99. The technician diagnoses the system and gives upfront repair pricing before work begins. The diagnostic fee is waived if the recommended repair is completed with MD Cold Services. Additional charges may apply for after-hours or emergency service.

## Q: How do I know if I should repair or replace my AC system?
Service: AC Installation & Replacement (MD Cold Services)
A: Replacement may be worth considering if an older system needs frequent repairs, has a major component failure, no longer cools effectively, or is becoming increasingly inefficient. MD Cold Services evaluates the existing system and explains the options.

## Q: How do you determine what size AC system I need?
Service: AC Installation & Replacement (MD Cold Services)
A: AC sizing depends on factors including square footage, layout, insulation, windows, ductwork and cooling needs. MD Cold Services evaluates the property and the system before recommending equipment.

## Q: Do you install energy-efficient AC systems?
Service: AC Installation & Replacement (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. MD Cold Services services most major residential and commercial HVAC brands, including Carrier, Rheem, Trane, Goodman, Daikin, Mitsubishi, Lennox, and others. (The site's answer names brands rather than efficiency ratings; the page also lists "High-Efficiency AC System Options Available".)

## Q: Do you offer financing for a new AC system?
Service: AC Installation & Replacement (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. MD Cold Services offers financing for qualified customers to make installation and replacement more manageable. Partners are Synchrony, Ygrene and Wisetack, subject to approval.

## Q: How do I know if my ductwork needs repair?
Service: Ductwork (MD Cold Services)
A: Common signs include weak airflow, uneven room temperatures, excess dust, visible duct damage, unusually high energy bills, and difficulty keeping the property comfortable.

## Q: Can leaking ductwork affect my AC system?
Service: Ductwork (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. Leaking ductwork can let cooled air escape before it reaches the rooms, which reduces comfort and efficiency.

## Q: Can ductwork be repaired, or does it have to be replaced?
Service: Ductwork (MD Cold Services)
A: It depends on the condition of the ductwork. Some leaks or damaged sections can be repaired or sealed. Extensively damaged or deteriorated ductwork may need partial or full replacement.

## Q: Can you improve airflow through existing ductwork?
Service: Ductwork (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. MD Cold Services evaluates the duct system and airflow to find leaks, damage, restrictions or other issues that may cause poor air distribution.

## Q: What is a ductless mini-split system?
Service: Mini Split Systems (MD Cold Services)
A: A ductless mini-split is an HVAC system that supplies heating and/or cooling directly to individual areas without traditional ductwork.

## Q: Where can a mini-split be installed?
Service: Mini Split Systems (MD Cold Services)
A: Mini-splits are commonly used in additions, garages, converted spaces, individual rooms and offices, and in other areas where independent temperature control or a ductless solution is wanted.

## Q: What is the difference between single-zone and multi-zone mini-splits?
Service: Mini Split Systems (MD Cold Services)
A: A single-zone mini-split serves one area with one indoor unit. A multi-zone mini-split connects multiple indoor units to provide individual temperature control in several areas.

## Q: Can a mini-split provide both heating and cooling?
Service: Mini Split Systems (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. "Many ductless mini-split heat pump systems provide both heating and cooling for year-round comfort."

## Q: What is a heat pump?
Service: Heat Pump Services (MD Cold Services)
A: A heat pump can both cool and heat a property. A heat pump cools in warmer weather and can reverse to provide heat when it is cooler.

## Q: Are heat pumps a good option for South Florida?
Service: Heat Pump Services (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. Heat pumps provide efficient cooling and also provide heating during cooler periods.

## Q: Can you repair an existing heat pump?
Service: Heat Pump Services (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. MD Cold Services diagnoses and repairs heat pump cooling or heating problems, refrigerant problems, electrical and component failures, airflow problems and other performance issues.

## Q: When should a heat pump be replaced?
Service: Heat Pump Services (MD Cold Services)
A: Heat pump replacement may be considered when the system is older, needs frequent or costly repairs, has a major component failure, or no longer provides reliable and efficient heating and cooling.

## Q: Why is attic insulation important in South Florida?
Service: Attic Insulation (MD Cold Services)
A: Attic insulation reduces heat transfer from the attic into the living spaces. This keeps the home more comfortable and lowers the AC system's cooling demand.

## Q: How do I know if I need more attic insulation?
Service: Attic Insulation (MD Cold Services)
A: Insufficient or uneven insulation, rooms that are hard to cool, excessive heat transfer from the attic, or higher cooling costs are signs that the existing attic insulation should be evaluated.

## Q: Can attic insulation help my AC work more efficiently?
Service: Attic Insulation (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. Reducing unwanted heat transfer into the home can lower the cooling needed to keep indoor temperatures comfortable.

## Q: Do you inspect the existing insulation before adding more?
Service: Attic Insulation (MD Cold Services)
A: Yes. MD Cold Services evaluates the existing insulation and coverage before deciding whether additional insulation or an upgrade is appropriate.
