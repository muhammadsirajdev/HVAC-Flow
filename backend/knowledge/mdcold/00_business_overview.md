# MD Cold Services – Business Overview

## Who MD Cold Services is
MD Cold Services is a licensed and insured HVAC contractor in South Florida. MD Cold Services provides residential and commercial heating, cooling and ventilation services. The homepage headline is "Miami's Trusted HVAC Experts." The company's tagline is "There when you need it most."

MD Cold Services serves three areas: Miami-Dade County, Broward County and the Upper Florida Keys.

MD Cold Services' social media profiles use the name "MD Air Conditioning" (Facebook page "MD Air Conditioning", Instagram @mdair_conditioning, TikTok @md.air.conditioning).

## Experience and track record of MD Cold Services
- MD Cold Services states "Over 16 years of professional HVAC experience serving South Florida" (also shown as "16+ Years").
- The About page states "12,000+" HVAC service calls completed.
- MD Cold Services shows a 5.0 / 5.0 Google star rating, labelled "Google Verified". The website does not state the number of reviews.
- MD Cold Services offers a "100% Satisfaction Guarantee". The website does not state the terms of this guarantee.

## Florida license of MD Cold Services
MD Cold Services holds Florida State License #CAC1824837 and describes itself as "Licensed & Insured". The license covers residential and commercial work ("FL LICENSE #CAC1824837 • RESIDENTIAL & COMMERCIAL").

## What MD Cold Services does
MD Cold Services offers two main service lines.

**Residential HVAC** (homes, townhomes and condos):
- AC Repair (24/7 AC repair and diagnostics)
- AC Installation & Replacement
- Ductwork Services
- Mini Split Systems (ductless)
- Heat Pump Services
- Attic Insulation

**Commercial HVAC:**
- Rooftop units (RTUs)
- Package units
- VRF/VRV multi-zone systems
- Commercial ductwork
- Commercial maintenance agreements

The homepage also groups "Attic Insulation & Air Quality" as a category. It lists "Blown-In Thermal Attic Insulation", "Air Duct Modifications & Sealing" and "Humidity Control & Filtration". The website has no separate page about humidity control or filtration.

## Why customers choose MD Cold Services (stated selling points)
The website lists these selling points:
- "Same/Next-Day Service": "Fast dispatch to keep your home cool in South Florida heat."
- "Upfront Honest Pricing": "No hidden charges. Clear quotes before work begins."
- "Licensed HVAC Contractor": "Highly skilled, background-checked professional technicians."
- "Residential & Commercial": "Equipped for single-family homes to large commercial facilities."
- 24/7 emergency HVAC service, "On-call & after hours".
- A stated commitment to "honest assessments, premium workmanship, and fast turnaround times".

## What the MD Cold Services website does not state
The website does not name the founder, owner or any team members. The website does not state a founding year, a street address or regular business hours.
