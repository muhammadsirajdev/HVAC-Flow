# MD Cold Services – Policies, Guarantees and Warranties

## MD Cold Services satisfaction guarantee
MD Cold Services advertises a "100% Satisfaction Guarantee" on its About page and on every service page. The website does not state what the guarantee covers, how to claim it, or any time limit.

## MD Cold Services pricing policy
MD Cold Services states "Upfront Honest Pricing" and "No hidden charges. Clear quotes before work begins." For residential AC repair, the technician gives upfront repair pricing after the $99 diagnostic and before work begins. For commercial work, pricing is confirmed before dispatch.

## MD Cold Services warranty on equipment and work
The AC Installation page says "Manufacturer Warranty Coverage Available on Select Systems." The Residential HVAC page mentions "warranty coverage" for installations. Not stated on website: warranty lengths, which systems qualify, or a labor/workmanship warranty from MD Cold Services.

## MD Cold Services cancellation and rescheduling policy
Not stated on website. The website gives no appointment cancellation, rescheduling or no-show policy.

## MD Cold Services refund policy
Not stated on website.

## MD Cold Services maintenance plan terms
See 05_pricing.md for all maintenance plan conditions. Key terms: one residential system per plan; additional systems must be at the same location; visits are in designated off-peak seasons; repair discounts cannot be combined with other offers unless stated; after-hours and weekend service is subject to after-hours or weekend pricing. Plan cancellation, renewal and contract length are not stated on the website.

## MD Cold Services privacy policy and terms of service
The website footer shows "Privacy Policy" and "Terms of Service" links, but their content could not be retrieved (no working page address was found). The website shows a cookie consent banner with the categories Functional, Preferences, Statistics and Marketing, and the buttons Accept, Deny and View preferences. The banner says: "To provide the best experiences, we use technologies like cookies to store and/or access device information."

## MD Cold Services licensing and insurance
MD Cold Services holds Florida State License #CAC1824837 (residential and commercial) and states it is "Licensed & Insured". Technicians are described as "background-checked".
